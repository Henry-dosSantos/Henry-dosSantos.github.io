## What it does

The goal is a tool that takes a cold plate design problem from geometry to a short list of candidate designs.

<Pipeline />

## Authorship

<Callout type="note" title="Who built what">
  The idea is mine, and I directed the work. The code was built with Devin, an AI coding agent, under my direction. The project started at the Cognition x HackerHouse hackathon in October 2026 on a team of 4.
</Callout>

## Status

In development. The hackathon version evaluates designs with a reduced-order thermal and hydraulic model and labels its results as low-fidelity estimates, not validated 3D CFD. It also includes a prototype dashboard for tracking OpenFOAM runs.

Designs from this tool have not yet been tested on hardware. The plan is to build them and test them on the [cold plate testbed](/projects/cold-plate-testbed/).
