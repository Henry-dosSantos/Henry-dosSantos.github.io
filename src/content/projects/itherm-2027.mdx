## Problem

Design a liquid cold plate for the ITherm 2027 student design competition. The plate has to hold a GPU and HBM under their temperature limits while staying under a pressure drop limit.

## Constraints

| Quantity | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| GPU temperature | ≤ 100 °C |
| HBM temperature | ≤ 70 °C |
| Pressure drop | ≤ 4 psi (27.6 kPa) |
| Scoring | Lower pressure drop scores better |

The numbers above are competition limits, not results.

## My role

I model the cold plate geometry on a team of 5. Prof. Joshi assigned the project as my METTL research work.

## Approach

Methods and tools: topology optimization, SOLIDWORKS and Fusion 360 for CAD, ANSYS Fluent for CFD, and Python and MATLAB for scripting.

## Prototype and test

The team is machining a physical prototype at Georgia Tech's Montgomery Machining Mall and testing it. The design will be backed by measured data, not simulation alone.
